# Villa paintings

Four original paintings generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-14.
Runtime assets are in `public/assets/paintings/`: mountain-lake.webp, coastal-harbour.webp,
floral-still-life.webp and woodland-path.webp. Each is 1024 pixels wide and shared across
existing frames. The frame mount preserves image proportions with no extra draw call.
Original full-resolution generations are retained in the Codex generated-images folder.
Existing frame destruction, wall support and replication remain in use.

## Generation prompts

Shared prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original fine-art painting texture
for a framed picture in a realistic villa game. Create ONE finished traditional oil
painting. Medium: convincing hand-painted oil on linen canvas, fine visible brushstrokes,
layered pigments, artistic detail, museum-quality naturalistic composition. Landscape 3:2
aspect ratio. Flat straight-on full-bleed reproduction of the painted image itself, edge
to edge. No frame, border, room, mockup, text, signature, labels or watermark. This must
look like an actual detailed oil painting, not vector illustration or simple geometric art.

Subjects:
- Mountain lake: A luminous mountain lake beneath jagged distant alpine peaks at dawn,
  dark pine trees framing the near shore, soft mist reflected in still turquoise water.
- Coastal harbour: An old Mediterranean fishing harbour at golden hour, small wooden
  boats, weathered pale stone houses and deep blue water, warm terracotta rooftops.
- Floral still life: An elegant still life of cream and muted coral flowers in an aged
  ceramic vase, a pear and a folded linen cloth on a dark walnut table, rich deep olive background.
- Woodland path: A peaceful winding path through an old woodland in early autumn,
  dappled sunlight, amber leaves, textured tree trunks and atmospheric depth.
