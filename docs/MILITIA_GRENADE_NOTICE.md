# Militia grenade arm motion

Source: **Militia (Grenade Throw)** by **jameslucino117**.

https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/militia-grenade-throw-2eb40068a6b54dd389137b888092688e

The supplied GLB identifies its license as **CC BY 4.0**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: sampled chest-relative arm directions, wrist displacement and calibrated
wrist rotation; cropped and retimed the throw; adapted the motion to the SWAT
skeleton using inverse kinematics. Source meshes, textures, leg motion and root
motion are excluded from the runtime asset. Preserve this credit with distribution.

Regenerate from the supplied source with `node scripts/prepare-militia-grenade.mjs`.
